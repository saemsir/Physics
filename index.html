04 / SYLLABUS

Interactive course map

JEE Physics ＋

Mechanics: units, vectors, kinematics, laws of motion, work-energy-power, momentum, COM, rotation, gravitation, fluids, oscillations and waves.

Electrodynamics: electrostatics, capacitance, current electricity, magnetism, EMI, AC and electromagnetic waves.

Modern: ray/wave optics, dual nature, atoms, nuclei, semiconductors and experimental reasoning.

NEET Physics ＋

Core: mechanics, thermal physics, oscillations/waves, electricity, magnetism, optics and modern physics.

Practice: NCERT-linked theory, formula recall, PYQs, graph reading and timed MCQs.

CBSE Physics ＋

Board focus: definitions, derivations, diagrams, numericals, NCERT exercises and structured revision.

Exam skill: present steps clearly, use SI units consistently, and check limiting cases where useful.
